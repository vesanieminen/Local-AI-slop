DINO BRAWL 3D

Pick an island, then choose your dinosaur and beat the challengers! There are thirteen to be: stompers, biters, horned chargers, one that spits gooey venom, a Brachiosaurus so tall it blocks the sun — and the Mosasaurus itself, if you fancy ruling the sea. Every expedition rolls a huge brand-new island with five challengers of its own: pine hills, crystal mesas, sunset lagoons or the raptor-only Raptor Winter — with mountains, shallow bays and proper deep ocean, and herds of animals roaming all of it. The plant-eaters just graze — they only ever fight back if you bite them first. The giants can't even see a raptor: play one, JUMP onto a giant's back with GRAB and peck it while it bucks and thunders trying to shake you off! Dinosaurs wade into water — but too deep drowns them, for you and for them (unless you ARE the Mosasaurus — the sea is your dinner plate). Hold GRAB next to a smaller dinosaur to clamp onto it and drag it around. Run out of hearts and your dinosaur takes a nap — press a button and it wakes up safe at camp. Dinosaurs of your own kind stay out of the fight — and you can switch dinosaurs, or move to a whole new island, anytime with the SWAP button. Up to four heroes can share one screen: pick 2P–4P below and each player gets their own pane, hearts and minimap.

PLAYERS

WASD / arrows — move  •  drag mouse or J/L — turn camera  •  SPACE / click — bite  •  hold G — grab & drag (a raptor: JUMP on giants!)  •  R — roar (a Dilophosaurus spits instead!)  •  M — mute  •  SWAP — change dino or world
2–4 players: P2 uses IJKL — move, comma/period — camera, O — bite, hold U — grab, P — roar. P3 and P4 each need a gamepad: stick — move, A/X — bite, hold RT — grab, Y/RB — roar
In the menus: swipe the dinosaur strip, arrows/WASD choose the island and dinosaur, Enter plays — the gamepad D-pad does the same
Gamepad: stick move, A/X bite, hold RT — grab & drag, Y/RB roar, Select/Back — menu, B — back, LB — mute, Start play, D-pad pick dino (up/down picks the island)
