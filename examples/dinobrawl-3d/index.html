DINO BRAWL 3D

Pick an island, then choose your dinosaur and beat the challengers! There are twelve to be: stompers, biters, horned chargers, one that spits gooey venom, and a Brachiosaurus so tall it blocks the sun. Every expedition rolls a brand-new island with five challengers of its own, so a new map always means a new cast. Dinosaurs wade into water — but too deep drowns them, for you and for them. Run out of hearts and your dinosaur takes a nap — press a button and it wakes up safe at camp. Dinosaurs of your own kind stay out of the fight — and you can switch dinosaurs, or move to a whole new island, anytime with the SWAP button.

WASD / arrows — move  •  drag mouse or J/L — turn camera  •  SPACE / click — bite  •  R — roar (a Dilophosaurus spits instead!)  •  M — mute  •  SWAP — change dino or world
In the menus: swipe the dinosaur strip, arrows/WASD choose the island and dinosaur, Enter plays — the gamepad D-pad does the same
Gamepad: stick move, A/X bite, Y/RB roar, Select/Back — menu, B — back, LB — mute, Start play, D-pad pick dino (up/down picks the island)
