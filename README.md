# Local AI slop 🧪

Browser experiments, served as GitHub Pages from one repo:

**▶ Hub: https://vesanieminen.github.io/Local-AI-slop/**

| Example | What it is |
|---|---|
| [Living room — WebGPU](examples/livingroom/) | Blender-exported living room at 160 fps with WebGPURenderer: AgX matched to the Cycles look, same HDRI, GTAO + bloom. Needs a WebGPU browser; ~50 MB. |
| [Orrery — the whole solar system, per pixel](examples/orrery/) | Real Keplerian orbits (J2000 elements) raytraced in one fullscreen shader: sun corona, Jupiter's Great Red Spot, Earth's clouds and night lights, Saturn's ring shadows. Compressed orbits to play, true scale to feel the void, time warp 1 hour → 20 years/s, tap a world to fly there. One file, no assets. |
| [Planetwalk — a new alien planet every tap](examples/planetwalk/) | An infinite procedural planet raymarched per pixel — warped continents, masked ridge mountains, a sea mirroring two moons — and the same noise runs in JS, so your feet find the GPU's ground. Six biome families, star-catalogue names, synthesized wind and footsteps. One file, no assets. |
| [RollerCoaster — Scream Machine Edition](examples/coaster/) | A coaster that designs itself — lift, drop, airtime hills, helix — gets validated by a physics sim (stalls and 4-G layouts get re-rolled), then you ride the survivor from the front seat: 240 Hz point-mass physics, seat-measured G-forces, hand-projected rails, synthesized clanks, wind and screams. One file, no assets. |
| [Black hole](examples/blackhole/) | Every pixel raymarches an actual photon orbit around a Schwarzschild black hole: gravitational lensing, Doppler-beamed accretion disk, photon ring. One file, no libraries, no assets. |
| [Ink — a fluid you can stir](examples/ink/) | A real-time incompressible fluid in WebGL2: advect the velocity field, solve pressure with Jacobi iterations, project it divergence-free, and carry glowing dye along. Drag to pour ink into the vortex; four palettes, stir, clear and pause. One file, no assets. |
| [Coral — reaction-diffusion](examples/coral/) | Gray-Scott reaction-diffusion on ping-ponged half-float textures: coral, mitosis, worms, fingerprints and mazes from six (f, k) presets. Drag to paint life; adaptive quality keeps phones at 60 fps. One file, no libraries, no assets. |
| [Monkey Island — 3D intro](examples/monkeyisland/) | The Secret of Monkey Island Special Edition opening rebuilt live: moonlit island, ships, glowing logo, and the Main Title Theme synthesized from inline score data. Click to start, drag to orbit. One file, no assets. |
| [Commander Keen — self-playing demo](examples/commanderkeen/) | The box art alive at 320×200 in the 16-color Keen palette: a bot runs, jumps and lie-flat-shoots through two looping levels — Vorticons, Vortiguns, ick, 1UPs, the grinning Vorticomander — with AdLib-style FM audio. Press any key to take over. One file, no assets. |

| [Dino Brawl 3D](examples/dinobrawl-3d/) | The fighting game for five-year-olds grows into a Three.js open world **many times bigger than the original island** with **fourteen fighters to be — thirteen dinosaurs and a Human**: pick your world at the start — Pine Forest hills, Crystal Canyon mesas and caves, a randomized Sunset Beach archipelago, or **Raptor Winter**, a snowbound map where nothing hunts but raptors — on **land much bigger and flatter than the old island, with mountains gathered into multi-peaked ridgelines instead of one lonely hump** (mountains, valleys, lakes, landmark names and challenger territories all reroll from a seed). Each world is ringed by a real coastline: walkable shallows give way to a deep ocean shelf, and small dinosaurs that paddle too far out drown — past chest-deep the sea takes land-dwellers, challengers included. The deep has an owner, but now it's selectable too: **be the Mosasaurus**, crawl pitifully over land or glide through the deep swallowing any small dinosaur that strays within reach, while a wild one patrols coasts you walk as a land-dweller — and both of you can **leave bait**: ROAR (or just time, for the wild king) drags a fat fish up onto the beach, and every animal on the island smells it from a huge way off and trots over to see what all the fuss is about. Beyond the five seeded challengers, **wild herds and packs roam the whole map** — grazers, raptor packs, even a Brachiosaurus — scenery with teeth that never counts against your goal. The Velociraptor's signature trick: **the giants can't even see a raptor**, so a raptor (wild or you) can leap onto <i>any</i> heavyweight — Triceratops, armoured Ankylosaurus, Spinosaurus, T-Rex, Stegosaurus, Diplodocus, Brachiosaurus or Distortus rex — peck away from the saddle, and hold on while the giant bucks, spins and thunders to throw the little terror off — plant-eaters still only fight back once bitten, but now they thrash wildly with a rider aboard. Every world also raises a **human village**: villagers tend the fire while spear-hunters war with any dinosaur silly enough to come close, and the food chain cuts both ways — hungry carnivores stalk the islanders and Mosasaurus snatch anyone wading too deep. Play as the **Human** and the village is your saddle shop: hold GRAB beside any dinosaur to **tame it and ride it**, steering it with WASD and sending it after whatever you BITE at — tamed giants never buck and never fear your roar. The in-game SWAP menu carries a mini map of the world you're standing on plus the picker, so you can move to a whole new map mid-game without losing your dinosaur, hearts or spot — one press grows the new island *and* hands you the dinosaur you just picked. Every species fights like itself: a frilled Dilophosaurus spits venom from the ROAR button, a Triceratops lowers its horns and charges, a Stegosaurus clubs sideways with its spiked tail, the huge long-necks answer with a ground-shaking stomp or a whole-body spin of the tail, the Ankylosaurus CLANGs head-on bites so come at its tail, Distortus rex ripples the ground before a stomp, and the Indominus stalks invisibly until it explodes at point-blank. **Up to four players share one screen**: 2P splits left/right, 3–4P quarters it, and each hero gets their own camera, hearts, roar bar, minimap and compass arrow — P1 on WASD + mouse, P2 on IJKL + O/U/P, P3 and P4 one gamepad each — while every dinosaur on the island turns toward whichever hero it can smell closest. Run out of hearts and your dinosaur topples over and naps where it fell (everyone else keeps playing), press a button and it wakes up safe at camp; when every hero is down… well, everyone naps. A gold compass arrow points at your next challenger; beat the five your world raised and it throws confetti and crowns you champion. Every species is a critter with authentic anatomy — sculpted in Blender (the .blend with its build scripts ships in the example) — on a jointed node rig animated live, with frills that flare and long necks that wave while they graze. Keyboard (1-0 picks a species), hot-plug gamepad (Select opens the menu) or big touch buttons; hearts, snack berries and a camp respawn keep it kid-gentle, and quality tiers keep phones smooth. |

## Layout

```
index.html            ← example hub (served at the repo root)
examples/<name>/      ← one folder per example
  static              ← plain HTML/JS → served as-is
  + package.json      ← CI runs npm ci && npm run build and serves its dist/
.github/workflows/deploy-pages.yml   ← builds all examples, deploys one artifact
```

Adding an example = new folder under `examples/` + a card in `index.html`.
Built projects must use relative asset paths (`vite base: './'`, and resolve
runtime URLs against `document.baseURI`) so they work under the Pages subpath.

## Living room example — local dev

```bash
cd examples/livingroom
npm install
npm run dev   # http://localhost:5173
```
